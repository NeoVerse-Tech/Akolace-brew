# AKOL'ACE BREW

> Revive Your Spirit. Reward Your Body.

AKOL'ACE BREW is a premium wellness-oriented beverage brand platform designed to combine modern beverage branding with structured digital preorder and delivery management.

The platform is evolving from a static promotional website into a scalable backend-powered operational system capable of handling customer preorders, logistics coordination, administrative tracking, and business analytics.

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

The current version of the platform focuses on:

- Brand presentation
- Product awareness
- Customer engagement
- Promotional experience
- WhatsApp-based preorder communication

The upgraded version introduces:

- Backend infrastructure
- Structured preorder workflows
- Delivery coordination
- Administrative dashboard systems
- Revenue and analytics tracking
- Operational scalability

---

# CORE OBJECTIVES

AKOLACE BREW aims to:

- Create a premium digital beverage experience
- Simplify preorder and delivery processes
- Enable supermarkets and distributors to order seamlessly
- Digitize operational management
- Build long-term scalable infrastructure
